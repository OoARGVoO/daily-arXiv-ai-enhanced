Method: 1. 利用Chandrasekhar-Detweiler关系推导归一化Wronskian之间的精确关系；2. 在缺陷位置r₀和扰动振幅ε的参数空间中，系统比较奇宇称和偶宇称扇区的准正模频谱演化；3. 分析排斥点分支在大r₀极限下的渐近行为和宇称相关修正项；4. 研究例外点（EPs）的位置、间距、临界振幅的指数衰减率及其宇称依赖性；5. 计算连接相邻QNM泛音的谱桥的弧长；6. 分析例外简并的宇称选择性及其对ringdown响应极点结构的影响。

Result: 1. Schwarzschild QNM等谱性在相同的非零局域缺陷扰动下不再保持；2. 奇偶宇称排斥点分支在大r₀时趋向相同的Schwarzschild QNM频率，首个宇称相关分离项出现在1/ℛ²阶（ℛ=r₀*/M为无量纲乌龟坐标）；3. 例外点形成两个具有相同渐近间距但相对位移的序列，位移由渐近Chandrasekhar-Detweiler因子的相位决定；4. 临界扰动振幅在两个扇区具有相同的指数衰减率，但渐近前因子的比值由该因子的模确定；5. 奇偶宇称EPs连接相同的相邻QNM泛音，对应谱桥的弧长几乎相同；6. 例外简并具有宇称选择性：在相同的(r₀,ε)值下，一对QNMs可能在一个宇称扇区的EP处合并，而在另一个扇区保持分离。

Conclusion: 研究揭示了Schwarzschild黑洞在局域缺陷扰动下奇偶宇称引力扰动的谱结构存在本质差异，尽管真空中两者等谱。通过Chandrasekhar-Detweiler关系，系统阐明了两种宇称扇区的例外点、临界振幅、谱桥结构的定量联系和差异。特别是例外简并的宇称选择性表明，在相同扰动参数下，准正模的合并行为依赖于宇称，这对黑洞ringdown信号的极点结构和引力波观测具有重要物理意义。

Abstract: The odd- and even-parity gravitational perturbations of a Schwarzschild black hole are governed by the distinct Regge-Wheeler and Zerilli potentials, yet their quasinormal-mode (QNM) spectra are identical in vacuum. Building on our previous study of the odd-parity Regge-Wheeler spectrum [M. Ould El Hadj and S. R. Dolan, arXiv:2608.16283], we investigate the corresponding even-parity Zerilli problem and compare the two sectors under the same perturbation of amplitude $ε$ at radius $r_0$. Using the Chandrasekhar-Detweiler relation, we derive an exact relation between the normalized Wronskians and show that Schwarzschild QNM isospectrality is not preserved under an identical nonzero localized defect. We find that the even- and odd-parity repelling-point branches approach the same Schwarzschild QNM frequencies at large $r_0$, with their first parity-dependent separation appearing at order $1/\mathcal R^2$, where $\mathcal R=r_{0*}/M$ is the dimensionless tortoise-coordinate location of the defect. The corresponding exceptional points (EPs) form two shifted sequences with the same asymptotic spacing in the tortoise coordinate, while their relative displacement is fixed by the phase of the asymptotic Chandrasekhar-Detweiler factor. The critical perturbation amplitudes share the same exponential decay rate in the two sectors, whereas the ratio of their asymptotic prefactors is fixed by the modulus of the same factor. We further find that the even- and odd-parity EPs connect the same neighboring QNM overtones, with nearly identical arclengths for the corresponding spectral bridges. Finally, the exceptional degeneracy is parity selective: at identical values of $(r_0,ε)$, a pair of QNMs may coalesce at an EP in one parity sector while remaining distinct in the other, leading to a corresponding difference in the local pole structure of the ringdown response.
