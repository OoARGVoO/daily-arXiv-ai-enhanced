### [1] [MagiCFirm: A Runtime for Magic-State Cultivation with Algorithm-Hardware Co-Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29267)
*Jubo Xu,Abbas B. Ziad,Prakash Murali,Hongxiang Fan*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出MagiCFirm，一个用于容错量子计算中魔态制备的可配置运行时系统，通过算法-硬件协同设计方法，结合两阶段早期逃逸方案和专用数据通路，在保持逻辑错误率的情况下将魔态制备时间减少最多39.3%，使魔态受限工作负载的整体应用运行时间减少约11%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 魔态培育（magic-state cultivation）是降低容错量子计算中非Clifford操作成本的有前景方法，但面临两大挑战：(i) 缺乏连接逻辑软件和物理控制的开源经典运行时层；(ii) 决定魔态就绪状态的协议特定决策存在延迟约束。需要解决这些实际应用中的瓶颈问题以实现魔态培育的实际部署。

Method: 采用算法-硬件协同设计方法。算法层面：开发两阶段早期逃逸方案，离线识别信息丰富的探测器子集，构建紧凑的解码问题，在运行时与完整解码并行执行部分解码，使高置信度尝试能在完整解码完成前提前推进。硬件层面：设计MagiCFirm可配置运行时系统，将离线编译的微程序与用于探测器构建、事件处理和协议控制的专用数据通路相结合，实现魔态培育的端到端执行。

Result: 在评估的各种配置中，MagiCFirm在匹配的逻辑错误率下将墙钟时间的魔态制备时间减少最多39.3%。对于代表性的魔态受限工作负载，这转化为整体应用运行时间约11%的减少。

Conclusion: 通过算法-硬件协同设计，MagiCFirm成功解决了魔态培育实际应用中的关键挑战，提供了开源经典运行时层和低延迟决策机制。该系统显著降低了魔态制备的时间开销，为容错量子计算中高效实现非Clifford操作提供了切实可行的解决方案，对推动容错量子计算的实用化具有重要意义。

Abstract: Magic-state cultivation offers a promising alternative for lowering the cost of non-Clifford operations in fault-tolerant quantum computing (FTQC). However, realizing cultivation in practice exposes two challenges: (i) the lack of an open-source classical runtime layer between logical software and physical control, and (ii) the latency constraints on protocol-specific decisions that determine magic-state readiness. To address these challenges, we adopt an algorithm--hardware co-design approach. At the algorithm level, we develop a two-stage early-escape scheme that identifies an informative subset of detectors offline, constructs a compact decoding problem, and performs partial decoding in parallel with complete decoding at runtime, allowing high-confidence attempts to advance before full decoding completes. At the hardware level, we present MagiCFirm, a configurable runtime that combines offline-compiled microprograms with dedicated datapaths for detector construction, event processing, and protocol control, enabling end-to-end execution of magic-state cultivation. Across evaluated configurations, MagiCFirm reduces wall-clock magic-state preparation time by up to 39.3% at matched logical error rate. For a representative magic-state-bound workload, this translates to an estimated 11% reduction in overall application runtime.

### [2] [MQSS-Selector: RL-Guided Pass Selection for an MLIR Compilation Pipeline](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30104)
*Andre Youssefi,Ercüment Kaya,Minh Chung,Jorge Echavarria,Laura B. Schulz,Martin Schulz*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个统一的基于学习的选择器框架,用于整合量子计算软件栈中的设备选择、编译器优化和任务调度等分散环节,通过强化学习和深度学习模型同时优化保真度、编译时间和调度延迟等多个目标。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前高性能计算与量子计算(HPCQC)基础设施日益融合,但现有的量子计算软件栈仍然是碎片化的,设备选择、编译器优化和任务调度等组件相互分离。由于NISQ时代的量子设备易出错且资源受限,需要专门的优化和拓扑映射才能获得足够的保真度,因此需要一个统一的框架来协同优化这些分散的阶段,以提高整体系统性能。

Method: 提出了一个统一的基于学习的选择器(learning-based selector)方案,利用强化学习(reinforcement learning)和深度学习(deep learning)模型将设备选择、编译器pass优化和作业队列调度整合到一个连贯的框架中。该选择器能够根据量子电路特征和设备条件动态适应,并可扩展以同时优化多个目标。

Result: 该方法实现了对保真度(fidelity)、编译时间(compilation time)和调度延迟(scheduling latency)等多个目标的同时优化,能够动态适应不同的电路特性和设备状态,将量子软件栈中原本分散的多个阶段整合为统一的优化流程。

Conclusion: 通过提出统一的基于学习的选择器框架,本研究成功解决了当前量子计算软件栈碎片化的问题,为HPCQC融合基础设施提供了一个能够协同优化多个目标并动态适应系统条件的解决方案,这对于提高NISQ时代量子计算系统的整体性能和实用性具有重要意义。

Abstract: High Performance Computing (HPC) and Quantum Computing (QC) systems are increasingly converging towards unified High Performance Computing-Quantum Computing (HPCQC) infrastructures, driven by a growing need to bridge classical and quantum workflows, which affects all levels of the system stack, from the hardware to compilers and runtimes, all the way to applications. However, today's QC devices are still in the Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) era, are error-prone and resource-limited, and therefore require specialized optimizations and topology mappings to achieve sufficient fidelity. This places special emphasis on proper compilation and optimization within the overall quantum software stack. Many existing stacks remain fragmented, with separate components responsible for device selection, compiler-pass optimization, and job queue scheduling. This paper proposes a unified, learning-based selector that integrates these disparate stages into a cohesive framework. Our proposed selector scheme leverages reinforcement learning and deep learning models that can be extended to simultaneously optimize multiple objectives -- such as fidelity, compilation time, and scheduling latency -- while dynamically adapting to circuit characteristics and device conditions.

### [3] [Learning and interpreting policies for simultaneous entanglement requests in quantum networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30157)
*Leon Rode,Sumeet Khatri,Supartha Podder*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出使用强化学习方法解决量子网络中的纠缠资源调度问题，通过双深度Q网络(DQN)结合消息传递神经网络(MPNN)训练策略，在多种网络拓扑下相比基线启发式方法可在低71%的链路激活概率下仍保持100%成功率，并探索了利用大语言模型提取可解释策略的方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 未来量子网络需要同时在不同区域执行多项任务(如长距离量子信息传输、分布式量子计算、量子传感等)，这要求在最小化资源和延迟的同时，对链路级纠缠资源进行调度，并创建各种多体纠缠形式。传统启发式方法在资源受限情况下性能不足，因此需要开发更高效的调度策略来优化量子网络资源利用。

Method: 将问题建模为马尔可夫决策过程(MDP)，采用双深度Q网络(Double DQN)结合消息传递神经网络(MPNN)进行策略学习。使用经验回放缓冲区和课程训练技术来提高训练效果。核心物理参数是链路级纠缠生成概率(链路激活概率)。此外，还引入了实验任务放置受限于特定硬件类型的约束条件。为解释学习到的策略，定义了行为度量指标，并使用大语言模型(LLM)从DQN策略的示例动作中提取新的启发式算法。

Result: 在一组物理相关的网络拓扑中，所提策略相比基线启发式方法，在链路激活概率降低最多71%的情况下仍能保持100%的成功率。在添加硬件类型限制的约束条件下,该策略在链路激活概率降低最多59%时仍能保持至少80%的成功率。通过LLM提取的启发式算法性能与DQN训练的策略相近,表明这是一种从大规模量子网络中提取可解释策略的有效方法。

Conclusion: 强化学习方法(特别是DQN结合MPNN)能够有效解决量子网络中的纠缠资源调度问题，显著优于传统启发式方法，尤其在资源受限(低链路激活概率)场景下表现出色。利用大语言模型提取可解释策略的方法展现了应用前景，为大规模量子网络中直接训练计算成本过高的情况提供了解决方案，有助于量子网络调度策略的实际部署和理解。

Abstract: Future quantum networks will make use of entanglement to perform numerous tasks, such as sending quantum information over long distances, distributed quantum computing, and quantum sensing. In general, these tasks will need to be performed simultaneously in various regions of a network, while minimizing resources and latency. We will thus require policies for scheduling link-level entanglement resources, and using the link-level entanglement to create various forms of multipartite entanglement required for every task. In this work, we address this problem using reinforcement learning. We formulate a Markov Decision Process for the problem and use double deep Q-networks (DQN) with Message Passing Neural Networks (MPNNs), experience replay buffers, and curriculum training to obtain policies. The key physical parameter is the probability of link-level entanglement generation, i.e., the link activation probability. We show that our policies maintain 100% success for up to 71% lower link activation probability than the baseline heuristics for a set of physically relevant network topologies. We then examine an additional constraint where experiment (task) placements are restricted to specific hardware types and demonstrate a similar advantage in performance over heuristics, with our policy maintaining at least an 80% success rate for up to a 59% lower link activation probability. Finally, we explore methods to interpret the learned policy by defining metrics enabling conclusions to be drawn about the model's behavior and by tasking a large language model (LLM) to derive a novel heuristic given example actions taken by the DQN-trained policy. We find that the LLM heuristic performs similarly to the DQN-trained policy in performance, indicating a promising method for interpretable policy extraction for large quantum networks, where direct training becomes computationally expensive.
